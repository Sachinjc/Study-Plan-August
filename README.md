## ✍️ Learning Approach

For each topic, I try to document:

1. **What I learned**
2. **How it works**
3. **Why it is useful**
4. **Implementation / Code**
5. **Time & Space Complexity** — whenever applicable
6. **Important notes or mistakes**
7. **What I learned from solving the problem**

The focus is not just on solving problems, but on **understanding why the solution works.**
